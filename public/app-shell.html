How OurHouse thinks

1
Grocery = need − stockEvery dinner's ingredients are scaled to that day's guest count and to what the dish has learned from your reviews, then added up for the week. Whatever's already in your pantry is subtracted — only the shortfall goes on the list.
2
It stays live and safeNew needs appear automatically. Removing outdated automatic items requires “Rebuild grocery list”, so one phone can never erase groceries another person just added. Bought and hand-added items are always protected.
3
Cooking closes the loop“Mark cooked” deducts that dinner's scaled ingredients from the pantry. “Finish shopping” adds bought quantities back in. Stock stays accurate without any manual counting.
4
Smart low-stock defaultsCondiments like pickle, achaar, chutney, ketchup and jam default to alert at just 1 jar, since they're refilled rarely. Staples like dal, rice and atta get a sensible bulk threshold instead — editable anytime.
5
Scanning is honest about certaintyBarcode matches from the free Open Food Facts database come pre-selected as clear. Receipt-photo lines the on-device OCR isn't sure about are flagged verify and left unticked, so nothing enters your pantry without your say.
6
Choose special meal daysMark days for a lighter menu. Recipes tagged “Light meal” appear first, and your regular library stays available.
7
No paid AI account neededCommon OurHouse commands run on the phone. More natural requests use the app's shared Cloudflare AI allowance after sign-in, so family members never need to enter or pay for an AI key.
8
Editor & viewer accessYour Google account keeps the same household and role on every device. New members start as Viewers until an admin promotes them to Editor in Settings.
