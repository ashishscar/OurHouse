SOP 01 · Start here

Create your household

You are starting a new family space.

In OurHouseOpen OurHouse → Continue with Google

5 steps
  1. 1

    Use your own Google account

    Open the public OurHouse link and choose Continue with Google. Use the account you want to keep for this family.

  2. 2

    Choose the kitchen

    Select Swaminarayan, Vegetarian or Vegan. The kitchen choice applies to the household. All three exclude eggs, meat, fish and seafood.

  3. 3

    Name the household

    Enter a recognizable family name and the usual number of people to cook for. These portions guide meal and grocery quantities.

  4. 4

    Create the space

    Choose Create my household. You become the Owner and decide who joins.

  5. 5

    Confirm the first screen

    Today should show your household name and Family synced. Add a recipe or pantry staples, then invite your family when ready.

Done when

Today shows the correct household. Your kitchen and portion defaults are set.

Already invited?

Use the invitation link and follow “Join your family” instead of creating another household.

Returning later

Open the same app and use the same Google account. The OurHouse icon and loading indicator can appear while the saved session loads. No OurHouse security code is required.

SOP 02 · Start here

Join your family

A family admin has sent you a private invitation.

In OurHouseOpen the invitation link

4 steps
  1. 1

    Open the complete link

    Use the link from the person inviting you. Keep the full link intact; do not remove the text after the # character.

  2. 2

    Sign in for yourself

    Continue with your own Google account. Each family member uses their own sign-in.

  3. 3

    Check the invitation

    Confirm the household name and the Member or Read only access shown on screen.

  4. 4

    Accept and verify

    Choose Accept invitation. Check that Today shows the expected household and shared information.

Done when

You can see the intended household under your own account.

Link will not work

Invitations are single use and expire after seven days. Ask the admin for a new link if it expired, was revoked or was already used.

Wrong account or household

Stop before accepting an unexpected invitation. Confirm the link and Google account with the person inviting you.

SOP 03 · Family

Invite people & manage access

You want to add someone or change their access.

In OurHouseHouse → Settings → Family & invitations

5 steps
  1. 1

    Choose the right role

    Select Member for people who will edit meals, shopping, pantry, chores and expenses. Select Read only for people who should review household information.

  2. 2

    Create an invitation

    Choose Invite a family member, then Copy invitation. Create a separate link for each person.

  3. 3

    Share privately

    Send the complete link directly to the intended person. Anyone receiving a valid invitation may try to use it, so keep it private.

  4. 4

    Confirm membership

    After the person accepts, return to Family & invitations and check the member list. The Owner can also grant Admin access to a member.

  5. 5

    Maintain access

    Revoke an unused invitation if plans change. Use the access selector or Remove for existing people, reviewing each confirmation before continuing.

Done when

The intended person appears with the correct access level.

Removing access

Removal blocks future household access; expense history stays. Previously downloaded or exported copies cannot be erased remotely.

SOP 04 · Meals

Choose your kitchen mode

Your family is setting or changing its recipe rules.

In OurHouseHouse → Settings → Meals & money → Kitchen mode

4 steps
  1. 1

    Agree on the mode

    Use the kitchen reference below to choose the household’s dietary setting.

  2. 2

    Set the preference

    Choose the mode in Kitchen mode. Check the people-to-cook-for default on the same settings panel.

  3. 3

    Review the library

    After a change, review existing recipes. Older recipes are kept for editing, even if they no longer match the new mode.

  4. 4

    Adapt carefully

    In a recipe editor, use Adapt draft to our kitchen mode for supported substitutions. Review ingredients and cooking steps before saving or planning it.

Done when

Recipe suggestions and new recipe edits follow the family’s chosen mode.

Kitchen reference

Choose a mode here to read its rules. This only changes this guide’s explanation.

Vegetarian with no onion or garlic. Dairy is allowed. Eggs, meat, fish and seafood are excluded.
Product labels still matter

A recipe check cannot certify every packaged ingredient. Review labels, substitutions and cooking instructions. Swaminarayan recipes exclude onion and garlic; a method depending on them may require manual editing.

SOP 05 · Personal

Choose & frame a cover photo

You want a personal cover that fits the screen.

In OurHouseHouse → Settings → This device & data → Choose my cover photo

5 steps
  1. 1

    Choose a picture

    Tap Choose my cover photo, or expand Your cover photo on Today and choose Change for an existing picture. Select an image from your device.

  2. 2

    Frame the preview

    Drag the photo to reposition it. Pinch, use the zoom slider, or tap + / − to show more or less of the image. The visible frame is the cover area.

  3. 3

    Reset if needed

    Choose Reset framing to return to the centered starting position. On a keyboard, arrow keys move the image and + / − zoom.

  4. 4

    Save or leave it alone

    Choose Save cover when satisfied. Cancel leaves the previous cover unchanged.

  5. 5

    Readjust later

    Use Adjust under the saved cover to reopen its framing. Use Change to choose another photo.

Done when

The Today cover matches the preview and remains after reopening on this device.

Personal and device-specific

Cover photos stay on this device for your account. They are separate from the family’s synced household records and readable household backup.

Image will not open

Try JPG, PNG or WebP and a file smaller than 20 MB. Very small images may look soft when enlarged.

SOP 06 · Meals

Find, edit & save recipes

You want to build the family’s reusable dish library.

In OurHouseRecipes → Add recipe

6 steps
  1. 1

    Start a recipe

    Choose Add recipe and type a recipe name. Keep recipe suggestions enabled if you want online results.

  2. 2

    Review a suggestion

    Select a matching result to load an editable draft. Suggestions come from Wikibooks Cookbook and follow the kitchen mode.

  3. 3

    Check the serving size

    Set Serves to the number of portions the listed ingredients actually make. This is the base for later scaling.

  4. 4

    Review ingredients and method

    Correct ingredient names, quantities, units and cooking instructions. Cooking steps and Notes & options are optional sections. Suggested amounts use rounded weights and common fractions. You can also enter a recipe manually or choose a starter ingredient list under Notes & options.

  5. 5

    Keep source credit

    Leave the source and licence attribution attached to an imported online recipe.

  6. 6

    Save and confirm

    Choose Save recipe. Open it from the Recipes tab to read ingredients and cooking steps. Use Edit recipe when you want to change the saved version.

Done when

The dish appears in the family library with correct ingredients and servings.

Search privacy

When online suggestions are enabled, typed dish names are sent to Wikibooks. Turn suggestions off if you prefer manual entry.

A recipe is rejected

Check for excluded ingredients and incompatible cooking instructions. Correct the draft rather than removing the household’s kitchen rules.

SOP 07 · Meals

Import & share a recipe

You have a recipe message/file, or want to send one selected recipe.

In OurHouseRecipes

5 steps
  1. 1

    Bring a recipe in

    Choose Import recipe from message or file. Paste the recipe text, or select a .json or .txt recipe file up to 100 KB. Include name, servings, ingredients and instructions where possible.

  2. 2

    Review the draft

    Choose Review editable recipe. Unstructured text may be kept in Notes for you to arrange. A link by itself is not a recipe import.

  3. 3

    Edit and save

    Check dietary compatibility, portions, units and method, then Save recipe. Importing is a review step, not an automatic final save.

  4. 4

    Choose one recipe to share

    Open the saved recipe and expand Recipe options. Choose Share recipe or Export recipe file. When opening a recipe from a meal plan, cooking amounts follow that day’s headcount. For multiple dinner recipes, Start cooking lets you choose a dish. Ingredients have a preparation checklist; Ready to cook opens one cooking step at a time.

  5. 5

    Check the outgoing content

    Review the readable recipe, including notes and source credit, before you send or forward it. The exported file can be imported by another OurHouse user.

Done when

An imported recipe is saved correctly, or a selected recipe is ready to send.

Sharing is deliberate

Recipe exports are readable by their recipients. They contain the selected recipe; a household backup is a different, broader export. This guide never sends a message for you.

SOP 08 · Meals

Plan dinners for the week

You are preparing the week’s dinner plan.

In OurHousePlan

6 steps
  1. 1

    Pick the week

    Use the previous/next week buttons. Confirm the date range before making changes.

  2. 2

    Set each day’s headcount

    Open Dinner details and use the people controls for days with guests or fewer people eating.

  3. 3

    Choose dinner

    Tap a day, then Choose a recipe. Select recipes and confirm the picker. Repeat for the days you want to plan.

  4. 4

    Use planning shortcuts if helpful

    Expand Plan the whole week for editable suggestions, Paste menu and Copy last week. Review the result before relying on it.

  5. 5

    Build the shopping needs

    Choose Build shopping list. Check the resulting Shop list against the plan, portions and pantry.

  6. 6

    Record cooking once

    After the meal is actually cooked, use Mark cooked in Plan. This updates the meal and deducts the stored ingredient quantities from pantry stock.

Done when

The right dishes, dates and headcounts are planned, and shopping needs are reviewed.

Undo is not a stock restore

Undoing a cooked status does not automatically put deducted ingredients back into the pantry. Correct pantry quantities separately if necessary.

Meal feedback

Open Dinner details for people, notes and dinner reviews. Reviews help adjust later portion estimates automatically. There is no percentage to set when adding a dish. Review quantities when your family’s needs change.

SOP 09 · Meals

Keep pantry stock useful

You are adding stock, correcting quantities or restocking low items.

In OurHouseHouse → Pantry

6 steps
  1. 1

    Pick a section

    Use Kitchen for food and cooking items, or Household for cleaning and home supplies.

  2. 2

    Add an item

    Choose + Add item manually. Enter a clear name, current quantity, measuring unit, low-stock level, category and section.

  3. 3

    Save realistic measurements

    Choose Save item. Use consistent names and units between pantry items and recipes so quantities are easier to match.

  4. 4

    Review scans

    Choose Scan receipt to use your camera or Upload image to select an existing receipt from your phone photo gallery. Receipt photos are read in overlapping sections, including long warehouse receipts. Use receipt AI is the recommended reader. On first use, agree to send the photo sections to Cloudflare; your account’s choice is remembered on this device. Turn it off for on-device OCR. The app flags differences from printed totals and unit counts, and converts repeated pack sizes into total stock weight/volume. Review the product count, photo and original text. Prices stay separate from stock quantities; correct placeholders and select products before adding them. Use Add a missing product for a missed line. Photo & scan details offers a JSON download containing your photo, recognized text and edited items. JSON organizes the result; it cannot repair blurry or unreadable print.

  5. 5

    Respond to low stock

    Use Low stock to review alerts. Add needed items to groceries or restock the pantry after buying.

  6. 6

    Reconcile after use

    Mark cooked meals only once. Periodically compare physical stock with the app and correct quantities if items were used outside a planned meal.

Done when

Pantry quantities and units reflect the stock actually available.

Scanning needs review

Scanner downloads or camera access may be needed. Poor photos and product databases can produce incomplete or incorrect results; manual entry remains available.

SOP 10 · Meals

Shop together & check off purchases

The family is checking needs or shopping from a shared list.

In OurHouseShop

6 steps
  1. 1

    Build and review the list

    Use the meal planner’s Rebuild grocery list, or type a name in Shop, such as Rice, 2 kg rice or ½ cup lentils. Check the preview, then Add. Quantity & unit is optional. Paste list can help bring in a shopping message.

  2. 2

    Check stores and quantities

    Review the Indian store and Supermarket groups. Edit an item or switch its store when needed.

  3. 3

    Coordinate the trip

    Use I’m going shopping if you want the household to know a shopping trip is underway.

  4. 4

    Mark actual purchases

    Tick each item only after it is bought. Check the remaining count and the name/time shown on checked items.

  5. 5

    Finish and reconcile

    Use Done shopping when the trip is finished. If prompted, review any bill record you choose to add. Update pantry stock to match what arrived.

  6. 6

    Tidy intentionally

    Use Clear bought when the purchased rows are no longer needed. For five seconds, Undo can reverse your own add/remove action if those rows have not changed since. An expired Undo or a newer family edit needs a fresh review. If sharing via WhatsApp, review the text and recipient before sending.

Done when

The remaining list reflects what still needs buying and pantry stock is reconciled.

Bought is not stocked

Treat shopping checkoff and pantry quantity updates as separate checks. Do not assume a checked item has the right physical quantity stored.

SOP 11 · Money

Record bills & settle balances

You are recording a shared cost or a payment already made.

In OurHouseExpenses

7 steps
  1. 1

    Create the right group

    Choose + Create a group, keep or edit the suggested household group name, select family members and add any guest names. Set the currency and split preferences, then Save group.

  2. 2

    Enter the bill

    Open the group and choose + Add expense. Enter description and amount, then check currency and who paid. Today’s date and the group’s equal split are supplied. Open More options only if you need a receipt, different date/category, notes or repeat label.

  3. 3

    Choose who shares it

    Review Shared with and the split method. Use Equally, Exact amounts, Percentages, Shares, Adjustments or Item by item as appropriate.

  4. 4

    Check the arithmetic

    Expand the share preview. Exact amounts must total the bill; percentages must total 100%. For example, a CAD 25.01 bill shared equally by two people is allocated as CAD 12.51 and CAD 12.50.

  5. 5

    Attach and save

    Use More options to attach receipts or notes if needed, review the shares, then Save. Reopen the entry and check the amount, participants and attachment list.

  6. 6

    Review balances

    Use Balances for who owes whom, Reports for summaries and Activity for history. Keep different currencies separate when reviewing balances.

  7. 7

    Record a real settlement

    Make the payment outside OurHouse first. Then use Settle up or Record payment, confirm payer, recipient, currency and amount, and save the record.

Done when

The bill appears once, the shares reconcile, and any recorded settlement matches a real payment.

Payments are records

OurHouse’s settlement feature records transfers; it does not move money between bank accounts.

Receipt attachments

Up to five attachments can be added to an expense. Photos are optimized for storage; PDFs are limited to 512 KB. Review uploads before saving.

Repeat labels

If you select a repeat option, verify future entries yourself. Do not assume bills will be automatically paid or scheduled notifications sent.

SOP 13 · Money

Split a water bill by days stayed

Residents stayed for different parts of a billing period.

In OurHouseHouse → Water bill splitter

5 steps
  1. 1

    Use the bill’s dates

    Enter the total bill amount and the actual billing start/end dates.

  2. 2

    Check the residents

    Include each person who stayed during that period. Add names if needed and exclude people who were not there.

  3. 3

    Correct each stay

    Adjust each included person’s from/to dates for moves in or out. Review the displayed number of days.

  4. 4

    Calculate and review

    Choose Calculate fair split. Compare the shares, people and total with the original bill.

  5. 5

    Save the agreed result

    Choose Save this split. Use Past splits to review saved calculations later.

Done when

The saved calculation uses the intended amount, dates and residents.

Check both records

A saved water split is a calculation record. If you also track it in an expense group, verify that the expense is recorded once.

SOP 14 · Your device

Install & test notifications

You want a Home Screen icon or optional background family updates.

In OurHouseHouse → Settings → Install & notifications

6 steps
  1. 1

    Install on iPhone or iPad

    Open OurHouse in Safari. Open Share (through More if your toolbar hides it), choose Add to Home Screen, enable Open as Web App if offered, then Add. Open OurHouse from that icon.

  2. 2

    Install on Android

    Open OurHouse in Chrome and use the browser menu’s Add to home screen / Install option, then follow the prompt. The app’s Install OurHouse button can also show installation guidance.

  3. 3

    Enable on this device

    From the installed app, open Settings and tap Enable / update notifications. Allow the device/browser prompt if you want notifications.

  4. 4

    Check quiet hours

    Choose whether to use Quiet hours · 22:00–07:00 on this device. If changing this preference, use Enable / update notifications to save it for this device.

  5. 5

    Send a test

    Tap Send test and check your device’s notifications. Delivery can take a few minutes and depends on connection, browser support, permissions and Focus / Do Not Disturb settings.

  6. 6

    Review each device separately

    Repeat setup on another phone/browser if you want updates there. Use Turn off to disable notifications on the current device.

Done when

OurHouse opens from the Home Screen and a test notification reaches the intended device.

What notifications contain

Family updates use generic lock-screen text rather than names, recipes or amounts. Scheduled meal/chore reminders are still in development.

No test received

Check browser/phone notification permission, quiet hours, Focus and network access. On iPhone/iPad, test from the Home Screen app, not an ordinary browser tab.

Browser instructions

Installation wording varies by device and browser version. Official Apple and Chrome instructions are linked in the reference section below.

SOP 15 · Support

Understand sync & resolve problems

A change is waiting, a screen is loading or something looks inconsistent.

In OurHouseToday status · House → Settings → This device & data

5 steps
  1. 1

    Read the status

    Family synced means confirmed household state is available. Restoring your household… is a startup check; let it finish.

  2. 2

    Check account and connection

    Confirm you used the same Google account and the expected family invitation. Reconnect if offline.

  3. 3

    Review pending edits

    Use the status Review button or Settings → This device & data → Review unsent changes. Read each waiting or conflicting change.

  4. 4

    Resolve deliberately

    Compare your draft with the shared version. Choose the offered resolution only after checking the details. “Keep shared version · discard this draft” discards that local draft.

  5. 5

    Verify before cleanup

    Use House → AI app care to see technical checks or send a fixed problem code. Technical performance reports contain only Fast, OK, Slow or Failed counts; turn them off on this device if preferred. It can protect supported entry tools, but new code defects need a tested release. Wait for confirmation before signing out or clearing browser storage. If startup reports a temporary problem, use Try again. Share a screenshot and a description with support if it persists.

Done when

The household and account are correct and changes are confirmed or intentionally resolved.

Offline copies

An offline copy depends on a previously loaded device and its saved sign-in state. Clearing browser data can remove local photos and unsent edits.

Read only access

If an edit is rejected as read only, ask the admin to review your role. Repeated tapping will not change your access.

SOP 16 · Support

Backups, privacy & account controls

You want a readable export or need to understand account data.

In OurHouseHouse → Settings → This device & data

5 steps
  1. 1

    Finish syncing

    Review waiting edits before exporting, switching accounts or removing data.

  2. 2

    Export deliberately

    Choose Export readable family backup and read the confirmation. This is a readable file of household records; keep it in a private location.

  3. 3

    Know the restore limit

    Whole-household backup import/restore is not available in the current user interface. Recipe JSON/text import is available separately.

  4. 4

    Use the same account elsewhere

    Sign in with the same Google account to restore your synced household on another device. The current account flow does not ask you to create an OurHouse recovery/security code.

  5. 5

    Review deletion before confirming

    Delete my OurHouse account has a separate confirmation. Owner deletion deletes the household and its records for everyone. Member deletion removes that account’s membership while shared financial history remains.

Done when

An export is stored privately, or account controls have been reviewed before action.

Privacy model

Household data uses service-managed encryption. The service operator can recover account keys. Cover photos stay on the device; exported recipes and backups are readable by anyone who receives them.

Support

Use the app’s Privacy and Help & support links for current policies. Never send passwords, sign-in codes or secret invitation links in a support report.

SOP 17 · Start here

A simple household routine

Use this as a daily and weekly operating checklist.

In OurHouseToday · Plan · Shop · House

5 steps
  1. 1

    Start the week together

    Confirm the kitchen mode and portions, review pantry stock, plan dinners, then rebuild and review groceries.

  2. 2

    Before shopping

    Check the shared list and coordinate who is going. Add unplanned needs with clear quantities.

  3. 3

    After shopping and cooking

    Check off purchases, reconcile pantry stock, record shared bills once, and mark dinner cooked after it is made.

  4. 4

    Keep the house moving

    Review this week’s chores and mark completed work. Family members with read only access can review the plan and ask an editor to update it.

  5. 5

    Close the week

    Review expense balances and real settlements, check unsent edits, review portion feedback, then plan the next week.

Done when

Everyone knows the current plan, remaining shopping, household jobs and shared balances.

This checklist is yours

Checks in this document record your reading/practice progress in this browser. They do not complete chores, save meals or change data in OurHouse. Reset them when you want to repeat the guide.

Who can do what?

OwnerCreates the household, manages access, can grant Admin access, and controls owner-level account deletion.
AdminManages the household’s members, invitations and kitchen settings, and edits everyday household records.
MemberEdits everyday meals, recipes, shopping, pantry, expenses and chores. Asks an admin to change household access or kitchen settings.
Read onlyReviews shared household information. Requests a Member role if editing is needed. Personal device setup remains separate.

This manual describes the released web app. AI household chat, Siri integration, automatic household backup restore and scheduled meal/chore reminders are not described as available features. Menu wording can vary slightly by device.
