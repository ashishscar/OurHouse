# OurHouse release 33

Receipt AI is the recommended pantry reader. The first scan requires explicit consent to Cloudflare processing; the account-specific choice is remembered only on that device. Declining or switching it off uses local reading. Signing out clears scan drafts/results; another account must make its own choice.

AI scans use up to four concurrent sections instead of two, a bounded input width, JPEG encoding with less upload overhead, and conservative horizontal cropping of dark surroundings. Sections keep full vertical coverage with overlap. No per-receipt latency guarantee is made.

Retail parsing handles negative coupon rows without adding them as stock, compares net amounts after discounts against the printed subtotal, supports split pack sizes, short product codes and repeated weighted produce, and excludes payment/rewards rows after the product footer. Boundary matching handles output reflow while keeping repeated purchases elsewhere. Quantity × unit-price inconsistencies are flagged per product. Uncertain results remain visibly flagged and require review; products are never selected or saved automatically.

Private customer images stay outside Git, fixtures and logs. These examples are evaluation inputs, not model fine-tuning data. The dedicated model has no write/deployment tools. Server authorization, membership rechecks, input/output limits, hashed daily budgets and full-image local fallback remain in force. The AI section allowance is 24, as is the per-user request/minute limit; daily budgets remain unchanged.
