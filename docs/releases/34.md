# OurHouse release 34

Floating sheets, consent dialogs, quick-add choices, shopping options, recipe suggestions, drawers and confirmation boxes use a denser blue surface, clearer boundaries and stronger page dimming. Light and dark themes retain the existing palette, with opaque fallbacks for reduced transparency and browsers without blur support. Popup controls have visible keyboard focus.

Receipt scanning progress is a compact centered modal, rather than a full page sharing the page background. Cancel and progress remain visible over the dimmed app. Full-page editors retain their existing layout.

The Liquid Glass stylesheet now has its editable source in ui/liquid-glass.css and is copied to public/ by the normal UI build. No data, authentication, receipt extraction or notification behavior changes.
